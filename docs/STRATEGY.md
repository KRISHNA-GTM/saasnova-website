# Strategy, positioning and personas

Sources: the 12 AWS Marketplace listings in `Ref-SN/`, the legacy site content, and web research
carried out on 2026-09-25. **[F]** = verified fact with a source, **[I]** = inference or recommendation.

## Positioning

**SaaSNova is an operator-led Cloud GTM execution partner for ISVs and channel partners scaling
revenue through hyperscalers.**

What that means on the site:

- **Execution, not advice.** We do the work inside AWS Marketplace, Partner Central and ACE:
  listings, co-sell opportunities, private offers, program submissions. [F: every listing]
- **Operators with inside knowledge.** Founded and run by former AWS GTM and technical leaders;
  Jen Dawson previously served as a Global GTM Lead at AWS. [F: listings, legacy about/case studies]
- **Tool-agnostic human layer.** Marketplace platforms automate listings, metering and CRM sync.
  They do not sit with AWS sellers, get ACE opportunities accepted or build a BOX Joint Business
  Plan. SaaSNova runs that layer, alongside platforms such as its partners SaaSify and WorkSpan. [I]
- **AWS-first, multi-cloud aware.** Programs are built around AWS; PALaaS coordinates Azure and
  Google Cloud motions. (The legacy site claimed "equal depth across all three"; the listings
  describe AWS-first, so the site now follows the listings.)
- **Credentials:** AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner (Sept 5, 2026), launch partner for AWS
  Marketplace Storefront (June 16, 2026), 12 listings on AWS Marketplace, women-owned business. [F]

Removed from the legacy site because they could not be supported: "the world's first operator-led
GTM execution engine" (Invisory and CONNACT sell comparable models [F]), "industry's only",
"guaranteed" SLAs beyond the listings' own wording, program outcome multipliers ("3–5X attributable
revenue", "2–4X field engagement") and the "Trusted by 100+ ISVs" logo wall.

## Competitive landscape (summary)

- **Tooling vendors** [F]: Tackle, Clazar, Labra, Suger, SaaSify, Automatum, WorkSpan. All lead with
  automation and are adding human service layers (Tackle Co-sell Managed Services, Labra "PDM on
  Demand", SaaSify managed desk).
- **Services firms** [F]: Invisory ("Partner GTM-as-a-Service", Microsoft-leaning), CONNACT ("Cloud
  Alliance Team as-a-Service", ROI calculator), Grnmrk (fractional alliances leader, public pricing),
  Fractivo (ex-AWS founder, 8+ week alliance listing), CloudX (6-month ISV accelerator).
- **Partners, not competitors**: Carahsoft, Ingram Micro, communities such as PCAN (with its
  SHE:can track) and Partnership Leaders.

Where SaaSNova is different [I]: the breadth of a named, productized catalog on AWS Marketplace (12
listings), staged programs with durations, BOX funding execution (under-served by services firms),
a founder who ran the motion inside AWS, and women-owned status (no rival found leads with it).
Risk: PDMaaS/PALaaS/PMMaaS overlap Labra, CONNACT and Invisory directly, so pages lean on
concrete scope rather than slogans.

Free Partner Central migration and PRM help are table stakes (WorkSpan, Clazar, Suger, Labra and
others offer them) [F]; the site treats PCMaaS/PRMaaS as entry points, not differentiators.

## Market context used in copy [F]

- Partner Central moved into the AWS Management Console (Nov 30, 2025): IAM access, SSO, account
  linking. PCMaaS copy avoids deadline language because shutdown dates are only partner-reported.
- PRM GA Jan 30, 2026; AWS says PRM "is now how we measure partnership success across Partner
  programs and benefits" (Apr 21, 2026). The July 31, 2026 date in the legacy article has passed;
  the article carries an update note.
- AWS Generative AI Competency is now the AWS AI Competency (Nov/Dec 2025).
- From May 1, 2025 only "Deployed on AWS" SaaS counts toward committed-spend drawdown. SaaSNova's
  professional services listings show "Deployed on AWS: No", so the site does **not** claim that
  buying SaaSNova draws down AWS commits (see OPEN-ITEMS).

## Messaging rules

1. Every claim about SaaSNova traces to a listing, the legacy site, or an approved testimonial.
2. No prices anywhere. Programs are "custom", "scoped in a strategy session", "bought through an AWS
   Marketplace private offer". PRMaaS/PCMaaS say "no cost for eligible partners".
3. The primary action is always "Book a strategy session" (Calendly). Secondary actions: "Request a
   private offer" (AWS Marketplace) and "Contact".
4. Testimonials: Simplesense first and most prominent, GuardDog second; wording exact.
