<p class="lead-para"><strong>NEW YORK, NY – June 16, 2026</strong> – SaaSNova, a women‑owned cloud go‑to‑market (GTM) acceleration firm, today announced its selection as a launch partner for the new AWS Marketplace Storefront, a feature designed to help AWS ISVs and channel partners expand their cloud revenue through simplified, branded marketplace experiences.</p>

<p>As a launch partner, SaaSNova will support AWS partners in the development and execution of their GTM co-sell campaigns, expanding their global marketplace business through their branded Storefront. Partners can showcase their solutions in private offer campaigns, streamline marketplace transactions, and scale co‑sell engagement with AWS field teams directly from their Storefront.</p>
