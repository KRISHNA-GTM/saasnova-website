<p class="lead-para"><strong>NEW YORK, NY – September 5, 2026</strong> – SaaSNova, an operator-led cloud go-to-market execution firm focused on AWS Marketplace and co-sell, today announced it has achieved AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner status within the AWS Partner Network (APN).</p>

<p>The designation validates SaaSNova’s proven track record of implementing and deploying cloud go-to-market programs that drive measurable pipeline and revenue for SaaS ISVs. It recognizes the company's sustained investment in AWS expertise, customer execution, and marketplace operations.</p>

<div>
<div>"Achieving AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner status directly impacts the speed and scale at which we execute for our clients. We built SaaSNova to close the execution gap for SaaS companies. Our customers need the Marketplace, partner, and co-sell work done. This milestone unlocks deeper AWS alignment, giving us an even stronger foundation to deploy revenue-generating programs for the companies we serve."</div>
<div>Jen Dawson</div>
<div>Founder & CEO, SaaSNova</div>
</div>

<h2 id="accelerating-execution">Accelerating Marketplace Execution</h2>

<p>The AWS Advanced Tier is awarded to partners who demonstrate real-world deployment experience, maintain certified technical professionals, and establish a track record of measurable customer outcomes. For SaaSNova's customers, this elevated partner status unlocks immediate, tangible advantages:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Accelerated Co-Sell Engagement:</strong> Direct access to AWS account teams and ACE collaboration to drive joint pipeline opportunities and align field sellers earlier in the deal cycle.</li>
<li><strong>Enhanced Funding Programs:</strong> Eligibility for AWS investment programs like the Migration Acceleration Program (MAP) and POC funding to de-risk cloud commitments and accelerate time-to-value.</li>
<li><strong>Priority Enablement:</strong> Early visibility into AWS go-to-market motions, accelerating SaaSNova's ability to deploy new marketplace features for ISVs.</li>
</ul>

<p>For many SaaS companies, getting listed on AWS Marketplace is only the beginning. Turning that presence into pipeline requires ongoing execution across Marketplace operations, AWS field engagement, and opportunity progression. SaaSNova operates as an embedded GTM execution team, actively deploying these motions to ensure ISVs are marketplace and co-sell ready in weeks.</p>
