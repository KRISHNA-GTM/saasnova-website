ISVs need a way for buyers to find the right products, understand the offering, procure through AWS Marketplace, and move into the broader sales motion. Channel partners need a way to bring those solutions into their own customer-facing sales motion and connect them to the Marketplace procurement process.</p>

<p>That is where our partnership with Carahsoft comes in.</p>

<h2 id="storefront">The Storefront is where the GTM motion comes together</h2>

<p>The AWS Marketplace Storefront gives ISVs and channel partners more than a listing.</p>
<p>It creates a branded procurement experience where buyers can discover and evaluate products, while ISVs and channel partners can connect their marketplace presence to the workflows that support the sale.</p>

<p>The strategic capabilities include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Unified co-sell & campaign-led GTM:</strong> Run joint GTM motions with channel partners through themed campaigns, co-branded landing pages, partner-attached solutions, and promotion of new solution themes.</li>
<li><strong>Channel partner campaign distribution:</strong> Create a campaign once and allow channel partners to insert it into their own storefronts with updated CTAs and lead routing directly to their sales teams.</li>
<li><strong>Multi-partner solution bundles:</strong> Showcase solutions alongside GSIs, channel partners, or complementary ISVs, creating opportunities for partner-attached deals and Channel Partner Private Offers (CPPO).</li>
<li><strong>Flexible kiosk template:</strong> A marketing-rich surface to promote launches, integrations, and joint solutions.</li>
<li><strong>Future analytics:</strong> AWS is actively building analytics for Storefront, including traffic, engagement, and drop-offs.</li>
</ul>

<p>That is the value of Storefront.</p>
<p>It unifies the buyer experience, procurement, private offers, partner workflows, and co-selling motion in one place.</p>

<h2 id="why-matters">Why this partnership matters</h2>

<p>This partnership represents a significant shift in how ISVs and channel partners approach the AWS ecosystem:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Carahsoft brings the ecosystem:</strong> Connecting ISVs with its broad network of technology vendors, resellers, system integrators, and channel partners, and supporting the Marketplace motion through its channel capabilities and DSOR model.</li>
<li><strong>SaaSNova brings the Marketplace GTM execution:</strong> Turning AWS Marketplace listings into actionable GTM and sales workflows.</li>
<li><strong>Storefront connects the experience:</strong> Bringing discovery, campaigns, procurement, partner enablement, and co-selling together in one place.</li>
</ul>

<p>For an ISV, this means AWS Marketplace Storefront becomes more than a place where the product is listed; it becomes part of the way the company is discovered, procured, sold through partners, and connected to the AWS ecosystem. </p>

<p>For channel partners, it creates a more structured AWS Marketplace storefront experience around the solutions they bring to their buyers, giving them a path to participate in campaigns, use their own storefront experiences, support partner-attached solutions, and participate in Marketplace procurement motions.</p>

<p>That is the opportunity behind the SaaSNova and Carahsoft partnership.</p>
