<p class="lead-para">Visibility is the new currency inside AWS.</p>

<p>By July 31, 2026, AWS will measure partner impact through Partner Revenue Measurement (PRM), a system that connects your product's consumption directly to AWS's internal scorecards.</p>

<p>This isn't just a compliance date; it's the moment AWS decides which partners stay visible and funded. If your PRM data isn't live, your co-sell engine loses visibility. AWS sellers can't see your impact, your product stops showing up in internal scorecards, and your opportunities stop getting prioritized.</p>

<p>PRM isn't paperwork. It's how AWS sees you.</p>

<p>And for most partners, it's the most important compliance milestone they haven't been briefed on yet.</p>

<p>Miss the deadline and the consequences are immediate:</p>
<ul>
<li>Loss of AWS Partner Funding Benefits (MDF/SDF)</li>
<li>Removal of the "Deployed on AWS" badge</li>
<li>Reduced co-sell eligibility with AWS Field teams</li>
<li>Freezing of your Marketplace and ACE motions</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-prm-measures">What PRM actually measures</h2>

<p>PRM moves AWS from estimated partner influence to real service-level attribution.</p>

<p>It tracks consumption across EC2, S3, ECS, RDS, Lambda, and more, creating a transparent, data-backed signal that tells AWS: this partner drives usage that matters.</p>

<p>AWS attributes revenue through three signals:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Zero-touch AWS Marketplace Metering:</strong> Automatic attribution for AMI and ML products</li>
<li><strong>Resource Tagging:</strong> Applying the <code>aws-apn-id</code> tag to cost-incurring AWS resources</li>
<li><strong>API Call Decoration:</strong> Embedding User Agent strings in your regular AWS API/CLI calls</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>

<p>Partners with active PRM data show up differently on AWS scorecards.</p>

<p>Field teams can see consumption tied to your product, strengthening co-sell alignment and giving Partner Development Managers the metrics they need to advocate for you internally.</p>

<p>Without PRM, AWS can't see your impact, and you lose visibility in every motion that drives growth.</p>
